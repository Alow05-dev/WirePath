Fishing fundamentals

📏 Depth matters

Start near the depth range where the target species and forage are most likely to overlap.

🚤 Boat vs shoreline

A boat opens deeper water, trolling lanes and mobile presentations. Shoreline fishing favors reachable structure and fan-casting.

🎣 Match the setup

The guide filters recommendations to presentations that make sense for your selected method.

🔁 Adapt one variable

If you aren't getting bites, change depth, speed, color or profile one at a time.
